Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions? We've got answers

How long does delivery take?

Within Lagos, delivery takes 24–48 hours. Nationwide delivery takes 2–5 business days depending on your location.

Do you offer warranty on products?

Yes! All products come with a minimum 6-month manufacturer warranty. We also offer extended warranty plans for added peace of mind.

Can I pay in installments?

Absolutely. We offer flexible installment plans on most products. Add an item to your cart and select "Pay in Installments" at checkout.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 7-day return policy on all products. Items must be in original condition with all accessories included.

Do you ship outside Nigeria?

Currently, we ship nationwide within Nigeria. International shipping is coming soon — subscribe to our newsletter for updates!
