Service FAQ

Common questions about our services

How long do repairs take?

Most repairs are completed within 24–72 hours. Complex repairs may take 3–5 business days. We'll give you an accurate estimate after diagnosis.

Do you use genuine parts?

Yes, we only use genuine, manufacturer-approved parts for all repairs to ensure quality and preserve your warranty.

Is there a diagnosis fee?

Diagnosis is free for products purchased from Numi. For devices bought elsewhere, a small fee applies — which is waived if you proceed with the repair.

Can I cancel a service plan?

Yes, you can cancel any service plan within 14 days for a full refund. After that, cancellations are pro-rated.
